First time using Article to Kindle? Setup Amazon delivery.

Add articletokindle@gmail.com to Amazon’s Approved Personal Document Email List. This approves the sending address; enter your Kindle destination address below.

Open Amazon Personal Document Settings

After signing in, open Preferences → Personal Document Settings → Approved Personal Document Email List → Add a new email address. This must match the local server’s SMTP_FROM address.

Amazon instructions

Send

Enter the Kindle email address from your Amazon account, not your Amazon sign-in address.
